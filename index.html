THE PROBLEM

Trimming shouldn't
wear you out.

String trimmers can become tiring to hold and control, especially during longer jobs. TrimWorks is designed around a simple idea: support the tool so the person doesn't have to carry all of its weight.

💪

Arm Fatigue

Holding a trimmer for extended periods can leave your arms and shoulders tired.

🧍

Back Strain

Repeated trimming can be uncomfortable when you're constantly supporting and maneuvering the machine.

⏱

Long Jobs

The longer you work, the more noticeable the weight and fatigue can become.
