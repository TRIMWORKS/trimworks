Trimming shouldn't wear you out.

Holding a string trimmer for a long time can become tiring. TrimWorks focuses on supporting the tool so you can focus on getting the job done.

Arm Fatigue

Holding the weight of a trimmer for long periods can make your arms tired.

Back Strain

Long Jobs

Large yards require more time and more effort. TrimWorks is designed with longer jobs in mind.

BUILT TO SUPPORT.

TrimWorks adds a support wheel to the trimmer so part of the tool's weight can rest on the ground instead of being held entirely by the user.
